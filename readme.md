1. Overview of invisible radio waves in the air. Untouchable. The original method of radio monitoring is to receive and measure the radio spectrum. Spectrum can tell people what frequencies have signals. However, the spectrum reflects very limited signal characteristics. Digital intermediate frequency radio receivers are used in modern radio management. Collect and analyze the signals at the baseband of interest. To identify signal characteristics. The radio frequency signal is generated by frequency conversion to produce a mid-frequency signal. After medium frequency band-pass filtering, Delivered to ADC digitalization. The digital waveform enters the digital signal processing module. In the digital signal processing module, the intermediate frequency digital waveforms are respectively multiplied by cosine and sine waveforms. Through low-pass filtering, Then you can restore the frequency that interests you.
The baseband signal of a point signal. Since the carrier frequency of the baseband signal is 0, its time-domain waveform manifests as a complex number I(t)+jQ(t), so it is commonly referred to as an IQ signal.
In the real world, radio signals are ubiquitous. Cognitive radios can perceive their surrounding environment and adjust their parameters based on the information in the environment to optimize service quality. The identification of the modulation type of signals has significant importance. Currently, it is not yet possible to achieve free communication at any time and place using radio waves, so continuous research into radio communication systems is necessary. Modulation recognition plays a crucial role in the cognitive understanding of radio communication systems.
